## 🪔 Why SmritiSetu?

In India, millions of families struggle to:

- 📅 Remember exact Vardhanti (death anniversary) dates in Telugu calendar
- 🕉️ Know the right tithi, nakshatram, masam for rituals
- 🧬 Preserve family history and ancestor stories
- 🌳 Visualize their family tree across generations
- 🔔 Get timely reminders for shraddha and rituals

**SmritiSetu solves all of this — in Telugu, English, and Hindi — on any device.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 Panchangam Calendar
Traditional Hindu Panchangam with tithi, nakshatram, masam, paksham, samvatsaram. View Vardhanti dates and upcoming rituals for all ancestors in one calendar.

### 🧬 Ancestors Management
Add complete ancestor profiles — name, relationship, gotram, family side, date of death, tithi details, favorite memories, prasadam details, and ritual notes.

### 🌳 Family Tree
Visual interactive family tree showing paternal and maternal sides. See your entire lineage at a glance.

### 🤖 AI Remembrance Messages
AI-generated heartfelt remembrance messages in Telugu, English, and Hindi for Vardhanti occasions. Share with family instantly.

### 🖼️ Memory Gallery
Upload and preserve precious photos and memories of your ancestors for future generations.

### 🔔 Smart Reminders
Automatic Vardhanti reminders — 30 days, 15 days, 7 days, 1 day, and on the day itself. Never miss a ritual again.

### 🕉️ Spiritual Quotes
Daily spiritual quotes from Bhagavad Gita and other scriptures in Telugu, English, and Hindi.

### 📲 Push Notifications
Browser push notifications for upcoming Vardhanti dates and ritual reminders.

### 🛡️ Admin Panel
Secure admin dashboard to view all users, ancestors, AI messages, and app analytics.
